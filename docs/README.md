# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's project management documentation. These guides provide standardized processes, templates, and best practices for delivering projects successfully.

## About OctoAcme's Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-centric project management methodology built on principles of clear ownership, data-driven decisions, psychological safety, and continuous improvement. Our approach emphasizes:

- **Customer-first thinking** — prioritizing customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery** — shipping small, testable increments rather than large monolithic releases
- **Clear ownership** — each project has named owners with defined responsibilities and decision-making authority
- **Data-informed decisions** — measuring impact and iterating based on evidence, not assumptions
- **Psychological safety** — encouraging feedback, experimentation, and learning across all teams
- **Transparency** — keeping stakeholders informed and aligned throughout the project lifecycle

## Project Lifecycle at OctoAcme

Every OctoAcme project follows a consistent five-phase lifecycle designed to reduce risk, improve quality, and ensure customer value:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business problem, align stakeholders, and secure go/no-go approval before investing significant resources
2. **Planning** — Scope work, estimate effort, define success metrics, and create a detailed release plan
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate on deliverables with continuous feedback
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with risk mitigation strategies, verification, and monitoring
5. **Close & Retrospective** — Capture learnings, celebrate successes, and identify improvements for future projects

Each phase has defined entry and exit criteria, key artifacts, and responsible parties to ensure consistency across teams.

## Key Principles in Action

Across all our processes, you'll notice three core principles in action:

1. **Clarity** — Every process defines clear inputs, outputs, and decision criteria to reduce ambiguity
2. **Efficiency** — We use templates, checklists, and defined workflows to streamline execution
3. **Continuous Learning** — We regularly review and improve our processes based on project outcomes and team feedback
